About the data & calculation Sources, coverage and limitations

Cutoffs use the workbook Rank column. Closing = maximum rank; opening = minimum rank, grouped by year, round, institute, course, allotted quota and allotted category. Counts represent allotment events, not unique candidates or seats across rounds.

Only each sheet’s current-round allotment columns are used. Earlier-round history, blank allotments and “No Upgradation” rows are excluded. A later round’s cutoff is not cumulative and does not represent the final admitted cohort. Category refers to the allotted seat, not the candidate’s personal category.

Spacing and punctuation variants in courses and institution addresses are combined. Degree types stay separate. Quota codes are expanded; management / paid / self-financed merit labels are grouped. Changed addresses are combined only when the normalized institution name, state and PIN code match exactly. Other address differences remain separate; inspect the address if names look similar.

States are extracted from institute addresses, including known state spellings and abbreviations. Missing states are recovered only when the same institution name has exactly one other source address with a known state; the remainder appear under “Unknown (not stated)”. This is the coverage of the supplied workbooks, not a complete catalogue of state counselling.

Historical allotments are a reference for comparing options. Verify admission decisions against the relevant counselling authority’s published result.
